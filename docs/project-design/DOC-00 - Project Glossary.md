# Document 0
# Project Glossary

> **Objective**
>
> Establish the official definition of all concepts used throughout the project, ensuring consistent terminology across the documentation, development, and maintenance of the automation.

---

# Glossary Principles

- Each term shall have a single official definition.
- No document shall redefine an existing term.
- Every new concept must be incorporated into this document.
- Definitions shall be independent of the technology used.
- Identification codes are permanent and must never be reused.

# 1. General Concepts

## GLO-001
### Automation

A system designed to autonomously perform repetitive tasks related to job searching while keeping the user responsible for all strategic decisions.

---

## GLO-002
### Job Posting

A job opportunity published by a job source and recorded within the system for processing.

---

## GLO-003
### Job Source

A platform, website, API, or any other source from which the automation retrieves job opportunities.

---

## GLO-004
### Professional Profile

The collection of professional information used to evaluate the user's compatibility with a job posting.

---

## GLO-005
### Compatibility

The degree of alignment between the user's professional profile and the requirements of a job posting.

---

## GLO-006
### Priority

The level assigned to a job posting according to the established evaluation criteria.

---

## GLO-007
### Application Package

The collection of resources prepared to support an application for a job posting.

---

## GLO-008
### Supporting Material

A document, analysis, or any other resource generated by the automation to support a job application.

---

# 2. Actors

## GLO-009
### User

The owner of the automation and the person responsible for all strategic decisions.

---

## GLO-010
### External Dependency

A system, service, or platform with which the automation interacts to obtain information or execute processes.

---

# 3. Functional Workflow

## GLO-011
### Functional Workflow

The ordered sequence of processes that a job posting follows from discovery to the completion of its processing.

---

## GLO-012
### Functional Process (FP)

A specific stage within the functional workflow.

---

## GLO-013
### Lifecycle

The set of functional stages that a job posting may pass through while it remains in the system.

---

## GLO-014
### Lifecycle Status

The current functional stage of a job posting.

---

## GLO-015
### Operational Status

The technical or operational condition of a job posting during processing.

Examples:

- Running
- Waiting
- Retrying
- Paused
- Error

---

# 4. Processing

## GLO-016
### Discovery

The process by which the automation identifies new job postings.

---

## GLO-017
### Preparation

The process of cleaning, normalizing, and validating the retrieved information.

---

## GLO-018
### Initial Evaluation

The process by which the automation calculates the compatibility level of a job posting by applying the defined rules.

---

## GLO-019
### Deep Processing

The process of performing a detailed analysis of a job posting to generate strategic information and supporting materials for the application.

---

## GLO-020
### Normalization

The transformation of information into standardized formats to facilitate processing.

---

## GLO-021
### Validation

The process of verifying that a job posting meets the necessary conditions to continue through the functional workflow.

---

## GLO-022
### Reprocessing

The re-execution of one or more stages of the functional workflow for a previously processed job posting.

---

## GLO-023
### Duplicate

A job posting that represents the same job opportunity as another posting already registered in the system.

---

# 5. Data

## GLO-024
### Input

Information received by the automation from an external source.

---

## GLO-025
### Internal Data

Information generated and maintained by the automation to control its operation.

---

## GLO-026
### Output

Information delivered by the automation as the result of its processing.

---

## GLO-027
### History

A chronological record of all events related to a job posting.

---

## GLO-028
### Traceability

The ability to reconstruct the complete journey of a job posting, including actions, decisions, statuses, and outcomes.

---

# 6. Decisions

## GLO-029
### Automated Action

An operational activity executed automatically by the automation without requiring a decision.

---

## GLO-030
### Automated Decision

A decision made by the automation using previously documented rules.

---

## GLO-031
### Recommendation

A suggestion generated by the automation to support the user's decision-making.

---

## GLO-032
### Strategic Decision

A decision reserved exclusively for the user due to its personal, professional, or legal impact.

---

## GLO-033
### Business Rule

A documented condition that determines how the automation must behave in a specific situation.

---

## GLO-034
### Functional Rule

A permanent rule that defines the overall behavior of the system.

---

# 7. Architecture

## GLO-035
### Process Engine

The component responsible for executing and coordinating the automation's functional workflow.

---

## GLO-036
### Decision Engine

The component responsible for applying business rules to produce automated decisions and recommendations.

---

## GLO-037
### Module

An independent functional component that implements a specific part of the automation.

---

## GLO-038
### Configuration

The set of parameters that determine the behavior of the automation.

---

# 8. Management

## GLO-039
### Audit

The system's ability to justify every action, decision, and status change through verifiable records.

---

## GLO-040
### Metric

An indicator used to measure the performance, operation, or quality of the automation.

---

## GLO-041
### Log

An event stored by the system to document the execution of processes, decisions, errors, or other relevant actions.

---

## GLO-042
### Error

A situation that prevents the normal execution of a process and requires a recovery strategy or human intervention.

---

## GLO-043
### Exception

An uncommon situation anticipated by the system's rules that modifies the normal processing flow without necessarily representing an error.

---

# 9. Opportunity Discovery (Module 1)

## GLO-044
### Corrida (run)

Execution instance of the Opportunity Discovery module, uniquely identified by `run_id`; every record generated by the module during a run is anchored to its `run_id` (RN-01).

## GLO-045
### run_id

Unique identifier of a run; mandatory traceability field in every record generated by Module 1 (events, sessions, offers).

## GLO-046
### session_id

Identifier of the platform session opened by Module 1 when the source entry succeeds; reusable by the subsequent nodes and registered in the session audit.

## GLO-047
### Lote (batch)

Group of offers captured together by the capture node of Module 1; each batch is materialized by the contract `capture_batch`.

## GLO-048
### set_indice

Index of the current filter set being applied to a source (0-based, in configured order); part of the traceability of every search and capture record.

## GLO-049
### Políticas de captura (capture policies)

Configuration parameters that govern the capture of offers: `max_paginas`, `max_ofertas_por_corrida`, `pausa_entre_lotes`, and anti-block strategy; effective per-source values merge with global defaults (RN-11).

## GLO-050
### Bloqueo de concurrencia

Persistent record (with `run_id` and `timestamp`) that guarantees only one active run at a time; its obsolescence threshold allows a stale lock to be overwritten.

## GLO-051
### Almacenes lógicos (logical stores)

Conceptual stores of Module 1 (Ofertas Totales, errores o sucesos, control de sesiones, corridas, bloqueo) persisted as tables of the same single SQLite database (D2).

## GLO-052
### entry_result

Contract delivered by the "Entrar a la fuente" node: `{estado, codigo_motivo, evidencia_acotada, numero_de_intentos}` without sensitive data.

## GLO-053
### search_result

Contract delivered by the "Aplicar los filtros" node, per filter set: `{estado, codigo_motivo, evidencia_acotada, ofertas_primera_pagina, estado_paginacion, total_declarado, set_indice, numero_de_intentos}`.

## GLO-054
### capture_batch

Contract delivered by the "Capturar ofertas" node with the captured offers batch and its capture accounting.

## GLO-055
### estado_captura

Contract of the capture state: page processed, accumulated captures, and whether the limit has been reached; consumes the pagination state of `search_result`.

## GLO-056
### Grupo A / Grupo B de códigos

Classification of failure codes in Module 1: Group A codes compromise the current source (it is closed or the run aborts); Group B codes are inherent to the filter set (the set closes and iteration continues with the next one).

## GLO-057
### Adapter (adaptador)

Component that encapsulates the specific access, search, and capture logic of each source platform in accordance with the official interfaces (INT-001/INT-003).
